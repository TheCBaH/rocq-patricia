## H1 — Primitive proofs

Dependencies: H0.G.

- [x] **H1.1** `HashTableBits.v` defines chunks, proves the per-chunk bound, proves a normalized 30-bit hash has zero chunk at depth six, reconstructs every normalized hash from its six routing chunks, and proves distinct normalized hashes differ at one of depths 0 through 5.
- [x] **H1.2** `HashTableBits.v` defines bounded popcount/rank and dense-list edits, proves bitmap occupancy is equivalent to the corresponding bit test for every native slot (soundness and in-range completeness), that enumeration is duplicate-free, and characterizes an empty bitmap as absent at every slot. It gives exact single-bit/full-bitmap popcount and rank at every native slot, an exact two-distinct-bit popcount, empty and full occupied-slot enumeration, enumeration length equal to bounded popcount, and proves low-bit masking is exactly the `< slot` filtered prefix of the original occupied-slot enumeration. Consequently rank is the dense length of that routing prefix; scan splitting and before/after-cutoff mask laws expose its exact workers, and every occupied bounded `N` slot is immediately after a prefix whose length equals rank. Adding a routing bit leaves that slot’s rank unchanged, and ordered two-bit routing assigns the left/right dense ranks zero/one. An absent bounded slot now splits the old enumeration at its rank and inserts exactly one element in the bitmap OR enumeration, yielding the matching popcount increment; a present slot has the dual exact deletion split and popcount decrement through `ldiff`. It also proves bounded lookup laws before/at/after insertion plus replacement/removal lookup preservation, insertion/replacement/deletion membership laws, exact valid-index removal cardinality, and slot/child paired replacement and insertion preservation.
- [x] **H1.3** `HashTableBucket.v` defines lookup/set/remove and empty/singleton/many normalization with pointwise update/removal preservation for different equivalence classes, exact hit/miss insertion cardinality, successful-removal cardinality, relation-parametric uniqueness preservation, and equivalent-query lookup congruence. `bucket_set_key_nodup` gives one all-cases update theorem for collision-key equivalence, requiring freshness only when lookup reports a miss.
- [x] **H1.G** Gate: primitive proofs compile and the theorem assumption audit passes (2026-09-15; 286 declarations closed under the global context).

## H2 — Source HAMT

Dependencies: H1.G.

- [ ] **H2.1** In progress: `HashTable.v` defines raw nodes, seeded tables and an independent flattened `bindings` view; `HashTableProof.v` now defines the hash/prefix, collision-uniqueness and bitmap/dense-child well-formedness invariant plus a public-root `table_wf` boundary tied to the table seed. Its prefix matcher records chunks from the root independently of current depth, so retained child prefixes follow the executable routing path. It proves normalization and same-hash removal preserve the collision portion, provides a complete unary-branch constructor from a nonempty well-formed child plus binding uniqueness, proves each well-formed branch has exactly one dense child per occupied bitmap slot, and proves every occupied slot has its routed nonempty/well-formed child at `dense_get (rank bitmap slot)`. `wf_branch_replace` preserves all branch fields for an exact valid replacement; `wf_branch_insert_absent` now proves the complete executable absent-slot insertion shape from a valid child and global binding uniqueness. It also proves every flattened binding of a well-formed tree carries the configured bounded hash. General branch preservation remains open.
- [ ] **H2.2** In progress: `HashTable.v` implements bounded `join_worker`; `HashTableProof.v` proves flattened binding union for both `join_two` and every fuelled worker path, including the fallback, proves that six-level routing of distinct bounded hashes does not take the fallback from the root or any shared routing-prefix suffix, and lifts a recursive equal-chunk child into a well-formed unary parent from its well-formedness alone (the worker is intrinsically nonempty and `wf` supplies binding uniqueness). It proves complete six-level root joins for every pair of distinct-hash well-formed non-branch nodes (leaf/leaf, either leaf/collision order, and collision/collision), and their direct diverging `join_two` paths using equivalence and hash congruence. New ordered `join_two` and non-fallback `join_worker` lookup laws return either inserted leaf at its own routed hash. `HashTable.v` proves a unary join `child_bit` is occupied exactly at its routing chunk, while every `join_two` bitmap is nonempty and bounded to 32 bits; differing chunks are occupied at both selected positions and absent elsewhere. Branch-subtree joins remain open.
- [ ] **H2.3** In progress: `HashTable.v` implements fuelled bitmap-routed `get_tree`; `HashTableProof.v` proves empty, matching/mismatching leaf and collision base cases, normalized same-hash collision lookup, zero-fuel branch fallback, and every one-step branch dispatch outcome under explicit bitmap/dense lookup premises. `get_tree_query_equiv` now proves relation-equivalent queries have identical lookup results at every tree/fuel depth; `get_query_equiv` lifts it through the seeded public API under equality reflection and hash congruence. `get_binding_iff` proves complete valid-map lookup semantics against the independent flattened binding view. For a well-formed branch, a present route is proved to dispatch to its exact nonempty well-formed dense child, so the dense-missing outcome is excluded; `get_tree_wf_no_fallback` proves public-depth lookup cannot reach the fuel-zero fallback. Other pointwise lookup laws remain open.
- [ ] **H2.4** In progress: `HashTable.v` implements persistent `set_tree` and the public seeded `set`; `HashTableProof.v` proves fuel-general `set_tree_wf_general`, public `table_wf_set`, and `table_wf_of_list`, so every valid bounded seeded update or bulk load preserves the complete invariant. It also proves `get_after_set_self` and `get_after_set_equiv`, returning the inserted value for direct and relation-equivalent queries under equality reflection, hash congruence and the bounded routed-hash contract. A present well-formed branch route dispatches to its exact ranked child and preserves parent validity from the recursively updated child; an absent route lifts a valid new leaf through the complete executable branch-insertion invariant. It proves distinct-hash leaf/collision joins, zero-fuel branch fallback, every direct branch dispatch outcome, `set_seed` base cases, and that valid routed control flow cannot reach the zero-fuel branch case. Other pointwise behavior and leaf-reversed/full collision joins remain open.
- [ ] **H2.5** In progress: `HashTable.v` implements persistent `remove_tree` and retains unary branches; `HashTableProof.v` proves fuel-inductive `remove_tree_wf` and public `table_wf_remove`, so removal preserves the seeded-table invariant for every valid table. It covers empty, leaf and collision cases, absent/missing branch routes, exact ranked-child recursion, singleton collapse and nonempty compact deletion. Dense deletion preserves binding uniqueness without a caller premise, and `bindings_remove_tree_in` proves every retained binding came from the original tree. Zero-fuel branch fallback, every direct branch dispatch outcome and `remove_seed` are also proved; valid routed control flow cannot reach that zero-fuel branch case. `get_tree_after_remove_self_wf` proves a self lookup is absent after removal for every valid tree under equality reflection, hash congruence and the routed key hash; `get_after_remove_self`/`mem_after_remove_self` and their equivalent-query variants lift that result through the seeded public API. Other pointwise lookup/removal laws remain open.
- [x] **H2.6** `HashTableProof.v` proves a well-formed branch starts strictly before the six-level bound, is impossible at or beyond that depth, and gives every stored child the next depth and its extended routing prefix. `branch_path_depth` proves every branch path has `depth + path_length < 6`; `wf_bindings_nodup` proves global flattened binding uniqueness modulo key equivalence.
- [ ] **H2.G** Gate: core laws kernel-checked; assumption audit and existing Patricia proof build pass.

## Deferred scope

CHAMP layout/refinement; skip/prefix nodes; custom 64-bit hashing; 32-bit OCaml
port; map/merge/union/fold extensions; ordered or
standard-library-compatible API; cryptographic collision protection; formal
expected-cost/allocation proofs; full OCaml heap/compiler verification; mandatory
physical identity reuse for unchanged updates. Each needs its own design and
acceptance criteria before adding it to the current release.
